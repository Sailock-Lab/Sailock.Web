Your vault is the encrypted file where Sailock keeps your passwords, notes and documents. It lives **only on your device**: there is no account and no cloud.

## Choose your master password

When you create the vault, Sailock asks for a **master password**. It is the only key to everything you store.

> **Important:** the master password cannot be recovered or reset. Sailock does not store it and has no server to ask for it. If you forget it, your data is lost for good.

A good master password:

- Is long: a phrase of several words is easier to remember than random symbols.
- Is unique: do not reuse a password from any other service.
- Is written down once, on paper, and kept somewhere safe.

## Confirm and continue

1. Type the master password.
2. Repeat it to confirm.
3. Read the warning and tick the box to confirm you understand there is no recovery.
4. Create the vault.

## Make a backup

Once you have a few entries, export a backup (`.slock` file) and store it somewhere safe. See *Export and import a .slock backup*.
